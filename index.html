01 About

I'm Jeffrey, a Frontend Developer who's genuinely happiest deep in the details of an interface — the kind of person who will spend an extra hour making sure a hover state feels right. I build pixel-perfect, fully responsive UI with semantic HTML, modern CSS (Grid, Flexbox, custom properties), and clean vanilla JavaScript, and I treat accessibility as a baseline requirement, not a checklist item bolted on at the end.

Most of that work has happened inside cross-functional product teams, sitting alongside designers, PMs, and backend engineers to turn wireframes into interfaces that actually hold up in production. I like the parts of the job that don't have a clean textbook answer — untangling a gnarly architecture decision, hitting a performance wall under real traffic, or shipping a feature without breaking three others — and I've built a track record of finding the version of the solution that survives contact with real users.

Away from the keyboard, I'm usually at the gym working through a program, or a few chapters into whatever I'm learning next — I've never quite been able to switch off the habit of getting a little better at something every day. Both of those habits sharpen the same three things I try to bring to every team: leadership, teamwork, and clear communication, because the best code in the world doesn't matter much if the people building it can't work well together.
